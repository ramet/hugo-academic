abstract: In recent years there has been a resurgence in direct methods to solve linear
  systems. These methods can have many advantages compared to iterative solvers; in
  particular their accuracy and performance is less sensitive to the distribution
  of eigenvalues. However, they typically have a larger computational cost in cases
  where iterative solvers converge in few iterations. We will discuss a recent trend
  of methods that address this cost and can make these direct solvers competitive.
  Techniques involved include hierarchical matrices, hierarchically semi-separable
  matrices, fast multipole method, etc.
