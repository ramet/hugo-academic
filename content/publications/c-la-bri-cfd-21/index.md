abstract: As the core of a large number of simulation tools, the resolution of large
  linear systems often represents the dominant part of the computing time. Massively
  parallel versions are needed to maintain advances in multi-physics and multi-scale
  simulations, especially when targeting exascale platforms. The aim is therefore
  to address the major challenge of designing and building numerically robust solvers
  on runtime systems that can scale up and push back the limits of existing industrial
  codes of the EoCoE project. In this talk, we will study the recent changes made
  to the solver with matrices issued from the project such as the block low-rank compression
  factorization, the capacity to exploit modern GPU accelerators through runtime systems,
  and the scalability on distributed memory.
